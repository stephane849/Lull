A calm, text-only YouTube Music client for E Ink phones, built for the Mudita Kompakt.

**Install:** download `Lull-v1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. If you installed a test build before, uninstall it first: this release is signed with a new key, so it can't install over test builds.

### What's in it
- **Home:** Pick up for the series you're in the middle of, and a short index (Podcasts, Search, Playlists, Liked, Offline). Hold the card to clear it.
- **Search:** YouTube Music (in sections, with More ›) or all of YouTube, heard as audio. No Shorts or live streams. Recent searches.
- **Lectures and podcasts:** resume where you stopped, Continue: Part 5, played marks, back 15 s / ahead 30 s, speed and sleep timer, oldest-first order.
- **Your library:** save albums, podcasts and playlists (including YouTube playlists); remove them, unsubscribe from artists, unlike songs.
- **Playlists:** add songs, make new playlists, reorder with Move to…, rename, delete. The queue can be reordered too.
- **YouTube links:** Share to Lull, or open YouTube links in Lull (turn on under Settings › Apps › Lull › Open by default).
- **Offline:** downloads, Wi-Fi only by default.
- **Android Auto:** Pick up, Offline, Podcasts and Playlists tabs, played marks, 15/30 skip buttons. Enable Unknown sources in Android Auto's developer settings.
- Always plays a video's original audio, never AI-dubbed tracks.

Lull is unofficial and uses YouTube Music's undocumented API, which is against YouTube's terms of service. Your account is your responsibility.
