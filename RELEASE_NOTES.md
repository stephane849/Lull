A calm, text-only YouTube Music client for E Ink phones, built for the Mudita Kompakt.

**Install:** download `Lull-v1.0.1.apk` below and open it on your phone; it installs over v1.0.0. Or add `https://github.com/stephane849/Lull` in Obtainium.

### What's new in 1.0.1
- **Home:** tap the Pick up / Now playing card to open Now playing. If it isn't playing yet, tapping it picks up where you left off. Hold the card to clear it, as before.
- **Now playing:** speed, sleep timer, download and like are icons now, so nothing gets cut off. Speed and sleep show their value under the icon once changed.
- **Now playing:** the time updates every second while playing, instead of every 15 seconds.

Lull is unofficial and uses YouTube Music's undocumented API, which is against YouTube's terms of service. Your account is your responsibility.
