A calm, text-only YouTube Music client for E Ink phones, built for the Mudita Kompakt.

**Install:** download `Lull-v1.0.3.apk` below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions. Or add `https://github.com/stephane849/Lull` in Obtainium.

### What's new in 1.0.3
- **Close the player:** the bar at the bottom has a × that stops playback and clears the queue, so the bar goes away.
- **Loading:** while a song is loading, the play button shows three dots and the player says Loading…; tap it to stop. If a song can't play, it says so, and play tries again.

Lull is unofficial and uses YouTube Music's undocumented API, which is against YouTube's terms of service. Your account is your responsibility.
