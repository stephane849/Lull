A calm, text-only YouTube Music client for E Ink phones, built for the Mudita Kompakt.

**Install:** download `Lull-v1.0.2.apk` below and open it on your phone; it installs over earlier versions. Or add `https://github.com/stephane849/Lull` in Obtainium.

### What's new in 1.0.2
- **Liked songs:** clearing them now sticks. Songs you unlike stay gone even while YouTube takes a moment to catch up, and a failed unlike only brings back that one song.
- **Now playing:** fits the screen with no scrolling. Add to playlist and More are icons next to speed, sleep, download and like, and Up next is one line.
- **Android Auto:** no more blank CD image; the player shows just the text.

Lull is unofficial and uses YouTube Music's undocumented API, which is against YouTube's terms of service. Your account is your responsibility.
